Research Interests

I’m currently working in demographic modeling, healthcare analytics, and population research. My work spans mortality modeling, forecasting, data harmonization, and statistical modelling across large administrative datasets. I am preparing to publish my Master's thesis regarding assortative mating and income inequality, comparing the United States and the Northern European countries (Denmark, Sweden, Norway) from 2000-2021.

I explore intersections between demography, economics, and AI, while building scalable data pipelines in R, Python, and SQL. My research interests include:

  • Fertility dynamics and dependency ratios
  • Assortative Mating
  • Mortality forecasting (e.g., Lee–Carter models)
  • Healthcare expenditure modelling
  • Longitudinal administrative data
  • Population health and policy analytics
