Welcome

Welcome! A little about me: I am a business intelligence analyst and data scientist focused on research in demography, population health, and environmental sustainability. My research focuses on income and wealth inequality, assortative mating, aging, mortality modeling, and population-health mechanisms. My work includes population forecasting, care burden measurement, predictive modeling, and real-time demographic data integration.
Technical stack: SQL, R, Python, Power BI, Tableau
10 years of experience in business intelligence, data engineering, health informatics

Education

  • MSc Global Demography from Universität Wien
  • MSc in Health Care Informatics from the University of Central Florida
  • BA in Philosophy from the University of Florida
