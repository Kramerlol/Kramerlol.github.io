Welcome

I am a business intellgience analyst and data scientist focused on research in demography, population health, and environmental sustainability. My research focuses on income and wealth inequality, assortative mating, aging, mortality modeling, and population-health mechanisms. My work includes population forecasting, care burden measurement, predictive modeling, and real-time demographic data integration.

This website is a collection of my research, projects, data tools, and writing.
