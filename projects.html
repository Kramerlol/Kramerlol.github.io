Preston Curve: Income, Education & Life Expectancy

An interactive R Shiny dashboard for exploring the classic Preston Curve and comparing income- vs. education-based explanations of cross-country life expectancy. Switch between GDP per capita (levels or log) and mean years of schooling, animate the relationship from 1960 onward, highlight individual countries, and experiment with a simple mediation model (Education → Income → Life Expectancy).

Tools & Data: R, Shiny, ggplot2, plotly, WDI, Wittgenstein Centre wcde
